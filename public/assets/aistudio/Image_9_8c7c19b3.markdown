## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for middle-and-high school students (classes 8–12), merging the intellectual rigor of a modern interactive science museum with the kinetic magic of an investigative time machine gadget. It rejects juvenile or cartoonish design in favor of rich, tactile, and intellectually inviting craftsmanship—treating teenage users with creative respect.

The visual style blends **Editorial Storytelling** with **Tactile Instrumental Minimalism**. Key tenets include:
- **Exhibition Craftsmanship:** Interfaces feel like curated physical gallery installations, utilizing parchment-warm canvas backdrops, high-contrast typography, and precision dials.
- **Instrumental Tactility:** Interactive elements feel like vintage-futuristic mechanical controls—time-scrubbers, decade sliders, schematic blueprints, and deep archival cutouts.
- **Curiosity-Driven Discovery:** High contrast between deep midnight navy ink and saturated raspberry pink cues exploration, pulling the student backward through eras of industrial design, materials, and manufacturing revolutions.

## Colors

The palette establishes an archival yet energetic aesthetic through an organic light canvas and deliberate chromatic contrast:

- **Primary (`#E11D48` — Raspberry Pink):** The focal temporal energy. Used strictly for chronometric actions, active timeline scrubbers, rewind triggers, interactive mechanical dials, and current era milestones. Hover and pressed variants shift from `#FB7185` to `#BE123C`.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` — Midnight Slate / Ink):** The anchor of narrative authority. Replaces pure black to deliver deep editorial clarity across headlines, high-contrast badges, structural framing, and primary text. Its lighter counterpart (`#1E293B`) supports secondary captions and metadata.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7` — Powdered Cyan / Blueprint Blue):** Evokes architectural schematics, technical cross-sections, and engineering notes. Paired with soft background tints (`#F0F9FF` and `#E0F2FE`) for exploded assembly views and scientific callouts.
- **Neutral (`#FAF8F5` — Warm Alabaster Canvas):** A museum-like paper surface that eliminates digital eye fatigue. Paired with card surfaces in `#FFFFFF` and archival paper tints (`#F6F4EE`, `#FDFBF7`, and warm cream highlights in `#FEF9C3`).
- **Structural Lines (`#E2E8F0` / `#CBD5E1`):** Calibrated warm slate hairline borders that hold modular exhibition panels securely without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes rhythmic, editorial headline forms against clean, technical UI legibility:

- **Headlines & Narrative Epochs (`Epilogue`):** Delivers sculptural, confident proportions reminiscent of high-end historical periodicals and scientific plaques. Its geometric weight anchors major time transitions and artefact titles.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Chosen for its warm geometric curves, open apertures, and superior legibility across dense assembly diagrams, historical notes, and step-by-step explanations.
- **Chronological & Blueprint Readouts (`Space Grotesk`):** Dedicated to technical epoch timestamps (e.g., `1884 CE`, `PATENT #441,029`, `REVS/MIN: 1,200`). Set in uppercase with tracked spacing to evoke calibrated instruments.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine utilizes a 12-column adaptive exhibition grid optimized for non-linear, temporal navigation:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** A 12-column layout with 48px margins and 24px gutters. Dual-pane view splits the screen into an interactive 3D/schematic temporal canvas (7 columns) alongside an archival research dossier and timeline dial (5 columns).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** An 8-column layout with 32px margins and 20px gutters. Dossier elements collapse beneath the primary interactive stage with persistent horizontal scrubber docks.
- **Mobile (Under 768px):** A 4-column responsive stream with 20px margins and 16px gutters. Features bottom-anchored, thumb-accessible temporal navigation dials.
- **Layout Rhythm:** Content modules conform to a strict 8px spatial grid, using `space-md` for standard metadata grouping and `space-xl` to segment distinct manufacturing eras.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth mirrors hands-on tactile museum pedestals and layered paper archives, rejecting diffuse drop shadows in favor of structured surface planes and tactile ambient offsets:

- **Level 0 (Gallery Bed):** The canvas base (`#FAF8F5`). Flat, warm, non-reflective.
- **Level 1 (Exhibition Plinth / Card Surface):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp 1px hairline border (`#E2E8F0`). Subtle ambient drop: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Artifact & Interactive Modules):** High-touch interaction cards and blueprint breakdowns. Enhanced offset: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`, framed with a 1.5px border (`#CBD5E1`).
- **Level 3 (Time-Displacement HUD & Modals):** Time-scrubbing controls, popover inspection loupes, and chronological selectors. Backed by frosted gallery glass (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85)` with `16px` backdrop-blur) and anchored with an elevated tactile shadow: `0px 16px 36px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined curvature language that communicates friendly mechanical design:

- **Standard Elements (Input, Chips, Buttons):** Configured at `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px), echoing smoothed industrial casing and instrument buttons.
- **Artifact Frames & Museum Cards:** Generously proportioned at 16px (`rounded-lg`) to 24px (`rounded-xl`), creating soft window portals into past manufacturing eras.
- **Instrument Dials & Knobs:** Fully circular (`rounded-full`) for chronological scrubbers, origin coordinate markers, and rotating lens toggles.
